Authoritative Travel Guides & Practical Advisories

  • Indian Army Permit Protocol: Indian citizens must carry original Voter ID Cards or Indian Passports plus 4 passport photos. Aadhaar Cards and PAN Cards are strictly NOT accepted by Indian Army checkposts for Restricted Area Permits (RAP). We prepare and submit all permit paperwork to the authorities; note that permit issuance remains subject to military checkpost protocols and seasonal weather conditions.
  • NH-10 Road Condition Advisory: The National Highway 10 follows the Teesta River gorge. During heavy rain or maintenance, traffic is smoothly routed via Panbu or Lava-Gorubathan. Our mountain drivers track real-time Border Roads Organisation (BRO) alerts.
  • Best Time to Visit: Spring (March to May) for blooming rhododendrons in Yumthang and Varsey; Autumn (October to mid-December) for crystal-clear 28,169 ft Kanchenjunga peak views.
  • Zero Direct Web Charges: We do not take payments directly on this website. All bookings are reviewed by our Gangtok desk to verify permit eligibility and seasonal road clearances before issuing an official proforma invoice.

Can foreign nationals visit North Sikkim and Nathula Pass?

Foreign nationals can visit Lachung and Yumthang Valley with a Protected Area Permit (PAP) booked through a registered agency in a minimum group of two. However, Gurudongmar Lake, Baba Mandir, and Nathu La Pass are strictly restricted to Indian nationals by the Ministry of Defence.
